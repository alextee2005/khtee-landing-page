Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you give me through this site. It is written in plain language rather than legal language.

The site is run by Alex Tee, based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. If you have a question about anything here, email alextee2005@gmail.com.

What I collect

Only what you type into one of the two forms on this site.

If you apply to the programme: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, current role, years of experience, what you wrote about what you want to change, and — if you chose to answer — how you found the page.

If you subscribe to the letter: your email address.

I do not ask for and never see your payment card details. Payment, when it happens, is handled by the payment provider on their own systems.

Why I collect it

  • To read your application and reply to you.
  • To contact you about the programme you applied for.
  • To send you the letter, but only if you asked for it.

I do not use your details for anything else, and applying does not sign you up to the letter — that is a separate tick box you have to choose.

Who else can see it

Three services process your information so that this site can work:

  • FormSubmit receives the application form and forwards it to my email.
  • Kit stores the newsletter list and sends the letter.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces this page uses, which means your browser contacts Google to load them.

Each of these keeps its own privacy policy. I do not sell your information, I do not share it for advertising, and I do not pass it to anyone else.

How long I keep it

Applications are kept while the cohort you applied for is running and for a reasonable period afterwards, in case you apply again. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

If you want your details deleted sooner, email me and I will delete them.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the letter using the link at the bottom of any email. It takes one click.
  • Ask what I hold about you, and I will tell you.
  • Ask me to correct or delete it, and I will.

Email alextee2005@gmail.com for any of these. You do not need to give a reason.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and does not track you across other sites. There is no analytics or advertising code on this page.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
